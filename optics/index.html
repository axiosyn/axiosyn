Critical-angle refractometry
of water, Intralipid and cream.

Four self-published papers by Ierotheos Katsoulis, from measurements made with a prism-coupling refractometer at the Electronic Devices and Materials Laboratory, University of West Attica. They cover polarization-resolved refractive indices of water, a polarization bias of the standard derivative method, instrument-corrected indices of turbid media, and what the same data can and cannot say about scattering.

Methods · 9 pages · start here

Apparent birefringence in critical-angle refractometry: a polarization bias of the derivative method

The standard derivative method makes isotropic water look birefringent by +3 to +8 × 10⁻⁵. The size of the artefact follows from the measured peak shapes alone; corrected, |nTM − nTE| < 2 × 10⁻⁵.

Turbid media · 7 pages

Complex refractive index of Intralipid and milk cream by instrument-corrected critical-angle refractometry

Intralipid-20 % at 25 °C: n = 1.3691, 1.3595 and 1.3515 at 532, 964 and 1551 nm (± 0.002, ± 0.002, ± 0.0008), with TE and TM in agreement. Milk cream does not yield a reliable index by this method, and the paper shows why.

Turbid media · 10 pages

Coherent scattering of undiluted Intralipid-20 % measured by critical-angle reflectometry

Fitting the whole reflectance edge shows that undiluted Intralipid scatters about half as strongly as scaled-up dilute data predict. It also shows the limits: n is good to about 10⁻³, k only to tens of percent, and the derivative method's k is biased low by back-scattered light.

Main findings

< 2 × 10⁻⁵

Upper bound on |nTM − nTE| for water at a prism interface, 532–1551 nm (95 % confidence).

+3 to +8 × 10⁻⁵

Apparent birefringence the derivative method reports for isotropic water. It scales with (np/n)² − 1.

± 5 × 10⁻⁴

Index offset set by the direction of table rotation. Averaging both directions removes it.

6.4 ×

How much the extinction coefficient would be overestimated if the artefact were read as absorption (water, 1551 nm).

1.3515

Refractive index of Intralipid-20 % at 1551 nm and 25 °C, relative to air (± 0.0008).

≈ 0.5 ×

Scattering of undiluted Intralipid-20 % relative to dilute measurements scaled up, at 964 and 1551 nm: the droplets are packed closely enough to scatter dependently.

up to 4 ×

How far the derivative method underestimates k in a turbid sample, because back-scattered light raises the edge. Its n stays within 5 × 10⁻⁴.

Data & code

The download contains the four papers, all figures, the complete analysis code (Python), summary result tables, and a representative sample of 16 raw scans. A short script runs the analysis on the sample and prints the results next to the values in the papers; single scans agree to about 1 × 10⁻⁴.

The full measurement dataset was recorded at the Electronic Devices and Materials Laboratory (EDML), University of West Attica. It may be the property of the laboratory and is not disclosed. The sample files are shared for verification only and are not covered by the open licenses below.
