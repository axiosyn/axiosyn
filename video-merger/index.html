Video Merger

Add two or more clips, put them in order, and get one video back. When the clips match, they are joined with no re-encode at all, so the result keeps the originals' exact quality.

Stays on your device

Your videos are never uploaded. All the work happens in your own browser, on your own computer or phone.

HOW IT KEEPS THE QUALITY

Every clip is checked first. If they all agree on video codec, resolution, pixel format, frame rate, pixel aspect ratio, rotation and sound, their compressed streams are joined untouched: nothing is decoded or re-encoded, and it finishes in seconds.

If they differ, each clip is encoded once to the same target, taken from the best clip present (its resolution and the highest frame rate), as H.264 at CRF 17 with AAC sound, and the parts are then joined. A clip without sound gets matching silence so nothing shifts. In a browser this is much slower than in a desktop app.

GOOD TO KNOW

Your files are only read, never changed, and nothing is sent anywhere; the page works offline once the engine is downloaded. Up to 50 clips per merge, and the same clip can be added more than once. AV1 clips can be joined only when they all match.
