QuestLife — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

QuestLife (“QuestLife”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) is a gamified habit-tracking mobile application developed and operated by Axiosyn, an independent developer. Axiosyn is the data controller responsible for your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use and protect it, and the rights you have over it.

If you have any questions, or wish to exercise your privacy rights, contact us at axiosyn@gmail.com.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect the data needed to run the game and your account. Specifically:

Account information

  • Email address — used to create your account, sign you in, and contact you about your account.
  • Password — stored only in a securely hashed form by our authentication provider. We never see or store your plain-text password.
  • Terms acceptance record — when you create an account, the date and time you accepted our Terms of Service and which versions of the Terms and this Privacy Policy were in effect.

Profile & gameplay data

  • Username (display name you choose) and your selected character class and pet companion.
  • The habits and quests you create, and your completion history (including focus-timer sessions).
  • In-game progress: level, experience points (XP), character stats, streaks, coins, diamonds, shards, potions, achievements, and encounter, dungeon, and bounty progress.
  • Purchase records (which diamond packs or QuestLife Plus subscription you bought) used to grant your items and prevent duplicate charges.
  • App preferences (e.g. sound on/off, reminder times, chosen background).
  • If you share your hero: a random link code and a snapshot of your hero’s appearance (see “Shared hero links” below).

Device & technical data

  • Notification permission status, used only to deliver the local reminders you choose to set. Reminders are scheduled on your device; we do not run a server that tracks your activity in real time.
  • Basic technical information necessary to operate the app and keep your account secure (such as authentication session tokens).
  • IP address — recorded in our backend provider’s logs when you sign in and use the app, for security and abuse prevention.
  • When the app checks for updates, our update provider (Expo) receives your IP address and an anonymous installation identifier so the right version of the app can be delivered to your device.

Purchases

QuestLife offers optional in-app purchases — consumable diamond packs and the QuestLife Plus subscription. If you make a purchase, the transaction is handled entirely by Google Play, with purchase validation by our provider (RevenueCat). We receive confirmation of your purchase and entitlement status (what you bought and whether your subscription is active) so we can grant your diamonds or Plus features, but we never receive or store your payment card details. Google Play handles all billing.

What we do NOT collect

  • We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, or files.
  • We do not use third-party advertising networks.
  • We do not embed third-party analytics or behavioural tracking SDKs.
  • We do not sell or rent your personal information to anyone.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and maintain your account and authenticate you.
  • To provide core gameplay — saving your habits, progress, and inventory, and syncing them across your devices.
  • To send the habit reminders you choose to schedule.
  • To keep the service secure, prevent abuse, and diagnose technical problems.
  • To respond to your support requests.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

4. How Your Information Is Shared

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with the service providers (“sub-processors”) that are necessary to operate QuestLife, and only to the extent required:

ProviderPurpose
SupabaseDatabase, authentication, and backend hosting of your account and gameplay data
Expo (Expo Application Services)App build infrastructure and delivery of app updates (receives your IP address and an anonymous installation identifier when the app checks for updates)
Google PlayApp distribution and, where applicable, payment processing
RevenueCatPurchase validation and subscription status. Receives your account’s user ID whenever you sign in, so the app can check whether you have QuestLife Plus, and records the purchases you make
ResendDelivery of account emails, such as sign-up confirmation and password reset (receives your email address)

Shared hero links

If you use “Share my hero”, QuestLife creates a link to a public page showing your hero. Anyone who has the link — on the web or in the app — can see your username, your hero’s appearance, and whether you are a QuestLife Plus member. They cannot see your email address, level, stats, habits, or any other account data. Each player has one link; sharing again updates what it shows, and a username change appears on it too. The link stops working when you delete your account. Only share it with people you are happy to show your username to.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to enforce our terms, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of our users or the public.

5. Data Retention

We keep your personal data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account (see “Your Rights” below), your account and associated personal data are permanently removed from our live systems immediately. Residual copies in encrypted backups are purged within 30 days. We may retain a minimal amount of information for longer only where required to comply with legal, tax, or accounting obligations, or to resolve disputes.

6. International Data Transfers

Your account and gameplay data are stored by Supabase in the European Union (Ireland). Some of our other providers (for example RevenueCat, Expo, Resend and Google) may process data in other countries, including the United States. Where data is transferred outside the European Economic Area, our providers rely on appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses to protect it.

7. Your Rights

If you are in the EEA or UK (GDPR)

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Delete your data (“right to be forgotten”).
  • Request a portable copy of your data.
  • Restrict or object to certain processing.
  • Withdraw consent at any time (e.g. by disabling notifications).
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

If you are a California resident (CCPA / CPRA)

You have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we collect and how it is used.
  • Request deletion of your personal information.
  • Correct inaccurate personal information.
  • Opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information — note that we do not sell or share your personal information.
  • Not be discriminated against for exercising your rights.

How to exercise your rights

You can delete your account and all associated data directly inside the app: open Profile → Delete Account, then confirm. For any other request, or to delete your account without using the app, see our Account & Data Deletion page or email axiosyn@gmail.com. We respond to verified requests within 30 days.

8. Data Security

We protect your information using industry-standard measures, including encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS), hashed passwords, and row-level access controls that ensure each user can only access their own data. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to protect your data and to respond promptly to any incident.

9. Children’s Privacy

QuestLife is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us at axiosyn@gmail.com and we will delete it.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the “Last updated” date above and, where appropriate, notify you within the app. The current version is always the one published on this page.
