Quests earn torches. Spend them on the rooms of the weekly dungeon, then face its boss: win the mini-games to land your hits before your hearts run out.

Add friends by code and take on duo and party quests side by side: walk 70,000 steps between you, keep a week-long streak together, or race through your quests as a party. Everyone who finishes gets the reward.
