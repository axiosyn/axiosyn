QuestLife — Licences & Attributions

Last updated: 3 August 2026

QuestLife is built with open-source software and licensed artwork. This page lists the third-party works bundled in the app and the licences under which they are used. Thank you to every author below.

Artwork

  • Character class & NPC sprites — licensed from craftpix.net under the CraftPix file licence, which permits use in commercial games. The assets are used as in-game graphics and are not redistributed as standalone files.
  • Pet companion art — original artwork created for QuestLife. © QuestLife.
  • UI pixel-art icons — original or licensed artwork bundled with the app.

Sound Effects

QuestLife's six sound effects — quest completion, critical hit, level-up, coin drop, item select and tab press — are released under CC0 1.0 Universal, a public-domain dedication that places no attribution requirement on their use. Each has been trimmed and converted to MP3 for the app. Our thanks to the creators who put this work in the public domain.

Bundled Mini-Games

Every mini-game in QuestLife is original work created for the app — “Sky Run”, “Blade Trial”, “Rune Lattice”, “The Performance” and the Focus Trial. © QuestLife. They use no third-party game engine and no bundled artwork: every frame is drawn procedurally on an HTML canvas.
